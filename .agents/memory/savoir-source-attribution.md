---
name: Savoir source attribution
description: Citation conventions for the French Savoir plant fiches and social-media context
---

Use the exact bibliographic title recorded in the research source registry for each scientific link shown in the app. Keep each note limited to what the cited source actually supports. Social posts may document reported practices, but must remain labeled as testimony and must not be treated as medical evidence or botanical identification.

**Why:** Shortened or invented citation titles make it harder for readers to verify a claim, while social snippets and lab studies can be overinterpreted as proof of local food practice or health benefit.

**How to apply:** When adding or editing Savoir fiches or articles, reconcile link titles and claim wording against `research/sources.json`; keep recipes out until a region-specific culinary source verifies them.
