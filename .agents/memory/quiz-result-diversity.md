---
name: Quiz result diversity
description: Product expectations for varied, deterministic plant-quiz recommendations.
---

**Rule:** Keep plant recommendations responsive to individual quiz answers. Identical full answer sets should remain deterministic; do not add randomness merely to spread results. Preserve the disclosure that recommendations are reflection prompts, not diagnoses or authoritative spiritual assignments.

**Why:** The owner observed that too many users received the same few associated plants. Averaging twenty answers into five dimensions loses distinctions between different response patterns.

**How to apply:** Keep question-specific plant-text signals and regression tests that verify distinct answer patterns—including patterns with equal five-axis averages—can produce distinct recommendations. Treat signal mappings as editorial heuristics and keep them reviewable.
