- URL : https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13409526/
- Extrait du résumé : Sweet potato leaves (SPLs), with an annual production of millions of tons, are rich in polyphenols and other bioactive components, yet remain largely discarded or underutilized.
